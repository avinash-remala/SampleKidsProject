High Pressure

💧

Liquid Water

Our atmosphere protects us from harmful solar radiation.

🌿

Home to millions of species of plants, animals, and microorganisms.

🧲

Magnetic Field

Earth's magnetic field protects us from the solar wind and cosmic radiation.

Fun Fact

Earth is the only planet not named after a god or goddess in Greek or Roman mythology.
